What it is, in plain words

Suppose several hospitals want the total number of cases across all of them, but none will hand its own count to the others. Or a group of companies wants an industry salary average without any firm revealing its payroll. Or federated phones want to sum a model update without any one phone's update being readable. The shape is always the same: combine many private vectors into one aggregate, and reveal only the aggregate.

The dangerous moments in that flow are two. First, the aggregate might be computed from too few contributors — a sum of one or two people is barely private at all, so there must be a floor. Second, a raw, un-masked vector might slip into the pile — and a single raw vector is the whole attack: it is the private data. Glue code gets both of these wrong constantly.

QuorumVeil is the envelope that makes both impossible by construction. Each contributor's vector is combined with a pairwise-cancelling mask — for every pair of contributors, one gets +r and the other −r, so across everyone the masks sum to exactly zero and vanish from the total, while each masked share on its own is offset. The unveil step then enforces the two refusals: it rejects any share not tagged as masked, and it refuses to produce a sum at all below a quorum of 3 live contributors. Every refusal comes back as structured data, never a warning string you could forget to check. The code a mutation gate proves is the code running under your clicks.

Honest limits — read this before you trust it

The masking and the noise in this kernel are a deterministic demonstration, not cryptography. The mask values are drawn from a tiny linear congruential generator modulo 101, seeded by the caller. That is there so the mutation gate can run the exact same numbers every time — reproducibility for the proof — and it is the opposite of what a real deployment needs. Anyone who knows the seed can recompute the masks, and the mask range is small. Do not use the bundled generator to protect real secrets.

What is real, and what this product is. Two things here are genuine, provable, and the point:

  • The cancellation is exact arithmetic. Pairwise masks sum to zero by construction, so the aggregate is the true sum (plus whatever noise you add) — not an approximation. The live demo shows the masked shares scrambled and the recovered sum landing exactly on the true total.
  • The two safety invariants are enforced and gated. "Never unveil below quorum" and "never pass a raw share into a sum" are properties of the code, proven by a 27/27 mutation gate. These are the glue-code mistakes that leak data, and here they cannot happen.

So the honest sale is this: QuorumVeil is the provable policy envelope you wrap around a production secure-aggregation or MPC library. Swap the toy generator for your field-size cryptographic masks and your calibrated differential-privacy noise; keep the quorum floor and the no-raw-release gate, which are the parts that are easy to get wrong and are proven correct here. It is the referee, not the vault.

In your own system

import { makeMasks, maskShare, unveil } from './kernel.mjs';

const vecs = [[10,2],[7,5],[3,9]];           // 3 private vectors
const { masks } = makeMasks(vecs.length, 2, 42);
const shares = vecs.map((v,i)=> maskShare(v, masks[i]).share);

const r = unveil(shares, 7, 0);              // seed, noiseScale
// r.ok === true, r.sum deep-equals [20,16] (the true column sums)
// unveil(shares.slice(0,2), 7, 0).why === 'below-quorum'

White paper — the short version

The problem. Private aggregation protocols are judged on their cryptography, but they leak in their plumbing: a cohort assembled below a safe minimum, or a single raw contribution that reaches the aggregate. These are policy invariants, usually enforced by hand-written glue that no one proves.

The claim. The two invariants — a minimum quorum of contributors, and never releasing a raw share — should be properties of a small pure function, provable and reproducible. QuorumVeil enforces both in unveil: it rejects any share not tagged masked, refuses to sum below a quorum, and returns every refusal as structured data rather than a warning. The masking arithmetic is pairwise-cancelling, so the aggregate is exact.

Why a mutation gate, not just tests. Passing tests prove the code works on the cases you thought of. A mutation gate proves the tests work: it rewrites the kernel 27 ways — flipping the quorum comparison, deleting the masked-tag check, breaking the pair loop — and demands the tests catch every one. 27/27 killed means the two invariants have no blind spot a change could hide a regression in.

What it is not — stated plainly. The bundled mask generator and noise are a deterministic demonstration for reproducible proofs, not cryptography: the generator is a tiny LCG mod 101 and the noise is not calibrated differential privacy. Do not protect real secrets with the bundled internals. QuorumVeil is the envelope — the quorum floor and the no-raw-release gate — proven correct, to be wrapped around a production cryptographic aggregation you supply. It is the referee for the policy, not the vault for the secret.
